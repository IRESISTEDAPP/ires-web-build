Common questions

I tapped the button and nothing happened

The buttons are held rather than tapped. Press and keep your finger down until the gold fills the whole circle, about two seconds, and you hear it land. Letting go early cancels it on purpose, so a moment is only recorded when you meant it.

How do I add or change my commitments?

On the home screen, tap the pencil under the main button, or go to Settings and choose Your commitments. You can rename them, remove ones that are not yours, and add your own.

How do I switch between commitments?

Swipe left or right across the face of the main button and the next commitment takes its place. The dots underneath show where you are, and you can tap them to jump.

What does the purchase include?

I Resisted is free to download, and free to use with all five commitments and every feature available until you have recorded 25 moments across three separate days. After that, a single payment of €4.99 keeps the app and everything already in it, with no limit on the moments you record. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and there is nothing else to buy.

I bought it before, on another phone

Open Settings and choose Restore Purchases, and it will come back as long as you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. The app also checks quietly each time it opens, so it usually restores itself without you doing anything.

Restore says no purchase was found

The most common cause is being signed into a different store account from the one that made the purchase. It is worth checking that first. If you are online, on the right account and it still cannot find it, email us and we will help.

Will I lose my history if I delete the app or change phone?

Everything is stored on the device itself, so it does not travel automatically. This version has no cloud sync and no backup or export, so if you delete the app you should assume the record is gone. On iPhone, a full encrypted device backup restored to a new phone may carry it across, but that is Apple’s own backup process rather than something the app manages, so it is not something to rely on. On Android the record is deliberately kept out of Google backups — that is what lets us say nothing about it leaves your phone — so a new Android phone starts with a fresh record. Your purchase restores either way, but restoring a purchase does not restore your history.

How is my streak worked out?

It counts days in a row on which you recorded at least one resisted moment, across all your commitments. A day you have not touched yet does not break it — the day is not over until it is over.

How do I start again from scratch?

Settings, then Reset history and commitments. It clears everything you have recorded and puts your commitments back to the defaults. It cannot be undone, so it asks first.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple and Google rather than directly with us, so refunds are handled by them. On iPhone, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the Play Store order history. If you have been charged and something has gone wrong, tell us as well and we will help you sort it.

Privacy

The app collects nothing at all. Everything you record stays on your own device. The privacy policy sets that out in full.
